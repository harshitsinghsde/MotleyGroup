Comprehensive Service Capabilities

Turnkey engineering, manufacturing, statutory compliance, and critical safety infrastructure.

1. Water & Wastewater Treatment Engineering In-House Manufacturing

ETP & STP Plant Fabrication

  • Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP): In-house design, sizing, and MS/FRP fabrication tailored to industrial COD/BOD loads.
  • Sewage Treatment Plants (STP): Custom biological systems based on MBBR and SBR technology.
  • Turnkey Execution: Complete civil, electromechanical installation, biological seeding, and commissioning.

Filtration & Softener Systems

  • Industrial RO Plants: Heavy-duty Reverse Osmosis plants for process and dialysis-grade applications.
  • Water Softeners: Fully automatic softeners for low-hardness boiler feed and HVAC chillers.
  • Pre-Treatment Filters: Dual-Media, Pressure Sand Filters (PSF), and Activated Carbon Filters (ACF).
2. Statutory Approvals & Environmental Clearances Compliance & Liaison

Pollution & Waste Clearances

  • UPPCB CTE & CTO: Consent to Establish and Consent to Operate Air/Water renewal processing.
  • Bio-Medical Waste (BMW): Authorization, barcoding setup, and annual compliance registers.
  • CGWA NOC: Central Ground Water Authority groundwater withdrawal processing.

Institutional Licensing & Legal Support

  • CMO Registrations: Chief Medical Officer hospital registration renewals and documentation.
  • AERB Approvals: Radiation facility approvals for radiology and diagnostic equipment.
  • Notice Support: Dedicated liaison and representation for regulatory show-cause notices.
3. Fire Protection & Electrical Thermal Diagnostics Critical Safety

Turnkey Fire Protection Systems

  • Fire Suppression: Hydrant piping, automatic sprinklers, and diesel booster pump room installation.
  • Gas Suppression: Clean-agent gas suppression systems for server rooms and LT electrical panels.
  • Statutory Fire NOC: Safety audits, flow testing, and staff evacuation mock drills.

FLIR Thermal & Power Audits

  • Infrared Thermography: Non-invasive FLIR thermal imaging of main LT panels, ICU/OT switchgears.
  • Early Hotspot Warning: Identifies loose connections before electrical fire outbreaks.
  • Electrical Certification: Earth-resistance testing (Ohms), phase-load balancing, and certification.
4. Edge AI Vision Labs Decentralized Visual Safety

Sub-5-Second Point-of-Origin Fire & Smoke Detection

  • 100% Local Privacy: Video stays strictly on local LAN; zero cloud dependencies or bandwidth lag.
