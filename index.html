Motley Group is the principal integrated environmental engineering, regulatory compliance, and critical safety infrastructure enterprise headquartered in Kanpur Nagar, Uttar Pradesh.

Operating across industrial, healthcare, and commercial sectors, Motley Group acts as a single, accountable shield. We safeguard core infrastructure through high-efficiency in-house water fabrication, zero-friction regulatory clearances, and smart early-warning hazard defense technologies.

Each division within Motley Group operates with dedicated technical expertise—under the overarching guidance of our engineering and compliance leadership—to ensure operational resilience and absolute statutory integrity.

Corporate Governance & Mission

"Our vision is to be the most trusted integrated environmental engineering and critical safety infrastructure enterprise—delivering sustainable water manufacturing, zero-friction regulatory clearances, and smart early-warning safety technologies under one accountable shield."
— Motley Group Vision Statement

Our Mission Philosophy is to ensure transparent, reliable, and accountable infrastructure management to protect the operational interests of all stakeholders, including industrial enterprises, healthcare providers, municipal bodies, and society at large. As a responsible engineering partner, Motley Group strictly adheres to statutory standards and environmental mandates.

Our Operational Framework is based on long-term industrial resilience. As manufacturing and institutional facilities become more complex and tightly regulated, it is imperative to build sustainable, self-reliant utility and safety operations.

Motley Group's execution scope is governed by four primary core pillars:

  • Water & Wastewater Treatment Engineering: In-house design, sizing, and fabrication of Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP), Sewage Treatment Plants (STP), and industrial RO/Softener filtration systems.
  • Statutory Approvals & Environmental Liaison: Comprehensive handling of UPPCB CTE/CTO consents, Bio-Medical Waste (BMW) authorizations, CGWA NOCs, CMO registrations, and AERB clearances.
  • Critical Infrastructure Protection: Turnkey fire protection systems, automatic sprinklers, gas suppression, and non-invasive FLIR infrared thermal diagnostics.
  • Edge AI Vision Labs: Sub-5-second visual fire and smoke detection running locally on existing CCTV infrastructure with instant alert integration.

ETP & STP Plant Fabrication

  • Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP): In-house design, sizing, and MS/FRP fabrication tailored to industrial COD/BOD loads.
  • Sewage Treatment Plants (STP): Custom biological systems based on MBBR and SBR technology.
  • Turnkey Execution: Complete civil, electromechanical installation, and commissioning.

Filtration & Softener Systems

  • Industrial RO Plants: Heavy-duty Reverse Osmosis plants for process and dialysis applications.
  • Water Softeners: Fully automatic softeners for low-hardness boiler feed and HVAC chillers.
  • Pre-Treatment Filters: Dual-Media, Pressure Sand Filters (PSF), and Carbon Filters.

Pollution & Waste Clearances

  • UPPCB CTE & CTO: Consent to Establish and Consent to Operate processing.
  • Bio-Medical Waste (BMW): Authorization, barcoding setup, and annual registers.
  • CGWA NOC: Central Ground Water Authority withdrawal processing.

Institutional Licensing & Legal Support

  • CMO Registrations: Chief Medical Officer hospital registration renewals.
  • AERB Approvals: Radiation facility approvals for radiology equipment.
  • Notice Support: Liaison and representation for regulatory show-cause notices.

Turnkey Fire Protection Systems

  • Fire Suppression: Hydrant piping, automatic sprinklers, and booster pump room setup.
  • Gas Suppression: Clean-agent gas suppression for server rooms and LT panels.
  • Statutory Fire NOC: Safety audits, flow testing, and staff evacuation drills.

Sub-5-Second Edge AI Hazard Detection

  • Zero New Hardware: Leverages existing RTSP camera feeds.
  • Instant Multi-Alerts: 3-5 second WhatsApp photo alerts + desktop sirens.
  • 100% Local Privacy: Streams processed locally with zero cloud dependencies.
